COMP 1001: Introduction to Programming

Interactive Workbooks

Read, predict, run, and check — all in your browser

Pranjal Patra · Department of Computer Science, Memorial University of Newfoundland

Each workbook pairs the lecture notes with live Python: every example runs, and every checkpoint and practice problem is checked as you go. Correct answers earn XP, which you can spend to reveal model answers. Progress is saved in this browser; use the ⋯ menu inside a workbook to export or import it.
